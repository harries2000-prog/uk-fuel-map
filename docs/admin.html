2. Choose the file

Fuel cards

Upload each card's site list as a CSV. It needs a heading row with a Postcode column; Site name, Surcharge and HGV (Yes/No) columns are optional. Uploading a new list replaces that card's old one.

Surcharges depend on who supplies the card, so they live in the Card providers tab.

Card images: tap the picture or Image. Best size 344 × 216 px (bank-card shape) – other shapes are cropped to fit. Cards without an image show a sample card.

Card providers

Surcharges depend on the company that supplies the card. Add each provider with its logo, then upload its surcharge list for each card. On the map, people pick their provider in the menu and see that provider's surcharges.

Surcharge list: your spreadsheet as-is (.ods, .xlsx or .csv) with a Postcode column and a surcharge column – Surcharge PPL in pence, or Surcharge (0.0125 = 1.25p); Site name and Group are optional. A site that takes the card but isn't on the list has no surcharge. Logo: any size, fitted into 240 × 120.

Brand logos

Pick a logo for any brand. Any size works – it's fitted into a 240 × 120 box automatically. Best results: a PNG with a transparent background, at least 240 px wide, trimmed close to the logo.
